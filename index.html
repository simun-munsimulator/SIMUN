MUN: Where Word Wars Prevent World Wars

Welcome to SIMUN, where we bring diplomacy to your fingertips. Beginners, enthusiasts and experienced delegates, this Model UN simulator helps you practice for conferences, explore various agendas, experiment with representing different countries, and hone your skills by competing with challenging AI players. The browser based experience mimics an actual MUN, with continuous score keeping and a race to win the title of best delegate.

The simulator is completely free to use. Just paste a Google Gemini API key to power the AI engine, set up your committee, and start playing. Your progress saves automatically, making it easy to work through a simulation over a few days at your own pace.
